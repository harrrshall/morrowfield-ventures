01 Approach

Small fund.
Clear terms.
Close work.

Morrowfield Seed Fund I backs twelve to sixteen companies. We usually enter before a priced institutional round and keep room for the next two financings.

Our work centres on customer evidence, distribution, operating cadence, senior hiring, and the next fundraise.

A

Local insight

A sharp understanding of how a market buys, pays, moves, or decides carries more weight than a broad category label.

B

Evidence of pull

Early proof can be a paid pilot, a repeat workflow, a waiting list with intent, or a product users keep returning to.

C

Practical ambition

We value founders who can hold a large outcome and the next six months of execution in the same conversation.

Our process

Every company gets the same four-step path. A partner stays involved from the first read.

  1. 01Read

    Deck, memo, product link, or customer note.

  2. 02First conversation

    Forty-five minutes with an investing partner.

  3. 03Working session

    Market map, product review, and reference calls.

  4. 04Decision

    A clear response with written context.
